> **Say goodbye to boring dark-mode boxes and futuristic neon clichés!**  
> **GitPie** transforms any GitHub repository into a delicious, interactive 1930s rubber-hose cartoon pie parlor inspired by the indie hit **Cuphead** and vintage Fleischer Studios cartoons.

---

## 🌟 What Makes GitPie Special

- **🥧 Rubber-Hose Cartoon Pie Engine:** Repositories are baked into golden pies with crimped cartoon pastry crusts, hand-inked comic outlines, and bouncy squash-and-stretch physics.
- **👀 Expressive Cartoon Eyes:** Pie slices sport classic 1930s Pac-Man cutout pupils that blink and track your cursor around the bakery.
- **🎲 Interactive Wheel Physics:** Grab and spin the pie wheel with tactile angular momentum and cartoon ticker sounds!
- **📜 Chef's Recipe Blackboard:** Click any slice to pop it out with a cartoon *"BOING!"*, inspect lines of code, pie share percentage, and explore ingredients (individual source files with direct links to GitHub).
- **🎶 Honky-Tonk Web Audio FX:** Procedural ragtime piano chords, cartoon spring boings, slide whistles, and vintage oven bells synthesized on the fly via the Web Audio API with zero external audio files.
- **🎞️ 1930s Film Grain & Vignette:** Creamy aged parchment paper, subtle vintage film reel flicker, and sepia camera vignettes.
- **⚡ Zero Build Dependencies:** Pure standard ES Modules and Canvas 2D. Runs anywhere with zero compile step or `npm install`.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. Explore Live on GitHub Pages
👉 **[https://sadmandied.github.io/git-pie/](https://sadmandied.github.io/git-pie/)**

### 2. Run Locally
```bash
git clone https://github.com/sadmandied/git-pie.git
cd git-pie
python main.py
```
*Opens automatically in your browser on `http://localhost:8080`.*

---

## 🎮 How to Play

- **Hover on a Slice:** Watch the slice squash and stretch outward with a cartoon squeak.
- **Click a Slice:** Launches cartoon crumbs and opens its source code recipe on the chalkboard.
- **Drag & Flick:** Spin the pie wheel!
- **Order a Fresh Repo:** Enter any `owner/repo` (e.g. `expressjs/express`, `facebook/react`) into the bottom oven console and hit **BAKE IT!** to watch the cartoon stove mix the batter!
